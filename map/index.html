🗺️ How Land Claims Work

JMC does not use a traditional claim plugin. Land is claimed by usage. Building, developing, or actively using an area establishes your claim to it. Be mindful of neighboring players and leave reasonable space around existing builds and projects.

If you are unsure whether an area is being used, talk to the nearby player or ask Staff before starting a large project. Do not build bases or farms in ways that disrupt special places such as spawn, shopping, or event areas, and repair damage you cause to another player's or public area in a timely manner.
