Spawn

A major public area. Avoid disruptive farms or private builds that interfere with spawn, event areas, roads, or other shared infrastructure.

LAND CLAIMS

Claimed by Usage

JMC does not rely on a traditional land-claim plugin. Building, developing, or actively using an area establishes your claim. Be mindful of neighbors and leave reasonable room around existing projects.

PROPERTY

Private vs. Public

Do not use another player's farms or take loot without permission unless the owner has clearly marked it Public.

DAMAGE CONTROL

Respect the Area

Repair damage you cause to another player's area or a public area in a timely manner. When you're unsure whether land is in use, ask before starting a large project.
