The JMC Handbook

Server Rules.

Use common sense, respect other players, and don't look for technicalities around the spirit of the rules.

🧠 Common Sense

  • The Loophole Clause: We are not a court of law. Technically following rules while constantly instigating, being passive-aggressive, ruining fun, or spreading constant negativity can still get you removed.
  • Respect: Light banter is encouraged; hate speech, slurs, and excessive harassment are bannable.
  • AI Usage: No AI Slop.

⚔️ PvP & Interaction

  • Consent is Key: PvP only when both players explicitly consent beforehand.
  • Grave Respect: Protect dropped items from despawning. Don't steal drops unless agreed upon.
  • Pranks should be funny and reversible. Griefing, stealing, destroying builds, and killing pets are bannable.

🛠️ Cheating, Mods & Exploits

  • No X-Ray, Fly, Kill Aura, or Baritone.
  • Litematica schematics and Easy Place are allowed.
  • Allowed dupes: TNT; gravity blocks; carpets, rails, and tripwire hooks. Item duping diamonds, netherite, or tools is a permanent ban.
  • Bedrock-breaking and auto-clickers are allowed.

📉 Server Performance & Redstone

  • Staff may remove machines that ruin TPS.
  • Nether Portal chunk loaders require Staff approval; Ender Pearl chunk loaders are allowed.
  • Contact Staff to whitelist ALTs.
  • You or your ALT may be kicked while AFK during high demand or lag; don't auto-reconnect after a kick.

🌍 Environment & Building

  • Don't disrupt spawn, shopping, or event areas with bases/farms.
  • Repair damage you cause to player or public areas promptly.
  • Land is claimed by usage; be mindful of neighbors.

💎 Economy & Progression

  • Don't use farms or take loot without permission unless marked Public.
  • Banned players' farms/builds may be auctioned by Staff.
  • Let new players experience the grind!

Build. Explore. Make it yours.
